# Guardian Health — Human / Connected

## Design exploration

The original page had scale and motion, but relied on purple gradients, stock-card compositions and invented metrics. The later page corrected content but repeated the same pale-blue panels, small labels, soft rectangles and side-by-side medical imagery. The staff UI repeated those treatments without enough separation between orientation, worklists and actions.

Three directions evaluated:

1. **Human / Connected** — warm ivory and charcoal, electric indigo, restrained apricot, editorial serif/sans contrast, panoramic human imagery and asymmetric chapter layouts. Distinctive in public, calm and legible in clinical work. Selected.
2. **Clinical Signal** — pearl, violet and graphite, technical grids and luminous imagery. Strong enterprise fit but too close to generic technology/SaaS branding.
3. **Care Architecture** — mineral white, vermilion and black, monumental typography and geometric cropping. Visually bold but too severe for patient-facing guidance.

Implementation keeps ASP.NET forms, IDs, request tokens, role routing, validation, services and real database content. Motion is progressive enhancement; clinical safety information is never an animated loading gate.

## Illustration provenance

`Assets/Images/guardian-human-care.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. It is illustrative and is never presented as actual Guardian Health employees, patients or facilities.

Prompt: Photorealistic cinematic panoramic hospital consultation-lounge scene: a mature female patient listening to a female clinician, with another care-team member softly out of focus. Warm ivory architecture, natural late-afternoon light, subtle warm wood, candid expressions, practical uniforms, neutral/charcoal/apricot tones and an indigo scrub top. Wide 21:9 framing, subjects central/right, architectural space left. No text, branding, medical readings, patient documents, staged smiles or floating UI. Natural anatomy and editorial photographic texture.
